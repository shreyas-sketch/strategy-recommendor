    Upload market data (TradingView export)

    Uses the app's data providers. Gives a regime history for every index even without 1-minute files.

    Upload strategy P&L

    A daily P&L table or a trade/order log (CSV or Excel). Needs a date column and a P&L column; a charges column, trade count or strategy-name column is used when present. The original file is kept unchanged.

    Strategies

    Backup & restore

    The backup holds the research database and every original uploaded file. Restoring replaces all research data with the backup's contents.

    Engine

    The main engine is used for the daily recommendation. Switch only if the walk-forward validation shows another method doing better over enough days.

    Daily publishing

    Trading days are weekdays not listed under Market holidays (Admin → Daily WhatsApp). If the app was down at the publish time, the pick is published as soon as it is back, until the 3:30 pm close.

    shrink_k: pseudo-days pulling a regime average toward the overall average · min_history: days before a strategy can be ranked · lower_quantile: conservative end of the range used for ranking · tail_penalty: weight on the average of the worst 5% of days.
